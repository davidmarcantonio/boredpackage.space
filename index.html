Why we're “boring.”

The platforms in this space are built and priced for enterprises with hundreds of locations. Most offices have one front desk and want it done right. “Boring” is how we keep it that way.

No mailroom software machine

No sprawling platform with a roadmap to justify. One app, one job: know whose box is at the desk. Nothing to integrate, nothing to babysit.

Nobody upselling you

No “locker integration” add-on, no per-recipient pricing, no account rep checking in. There's nothing else to sell.

99¢ for a reason

Simple software should cost simple money. No backend to run, no licenses per package, no data to monetize.

How it works

  1. Buy it

    99¢ on the App Store, or through Apple Business if you deploy iPads in bulk. One copy per front-desk iPad.

  2. Sign in with Google

    The log and the pickup emails live in your own Google Workspace, sent from your own account.

  3. Set it on the desk

    Lock the iPad to the app. Deliveries get logged, people get emailed, pickups get a time stamp.

We don't want your data. Honestly, it sounds “boring.”

A front-desk iPad sees who comes and goes. The only responsible design is one where that never leaves your own accounts.

  • We never see your packages, names or emails. The iPad talks to Google and nothing else.
  • The log is a Google Sheet in your Drive. Delete it and it's gone.
  • Pickup emails go out from your Google account, not from us.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking, no third-party code.

Why we can say this. There is no Bored Package backend. We couldn't collect your data if we wanted to, and we've designed it so we never want to.

99¢per iPad, once

Everything's included. Nothing's “exciting.”

  • Log a package in a couple of taps, photo optional
  • The recipient gets an email from your own Google account
  • Pickups logged with the time
  • Everything stays in your Google Workspace
